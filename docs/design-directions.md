# Direction exploration

GoShop joins sales channels through one inventory ledger. Staff work at a shop counter in ordinary daylight, often switching rapidly between stock and checkout tasks. The first screen must make sales, channel activity and stock exceptions understandable.

Predictable ruts: generic KPI-card dashboard; theatrical terminal counterpoint.

Candidate visual systems, ranked for this product:
1. Modern department-store operations desk: airy ledger tables, lavender chapter tabs, ink typography and readable transaction charts.
2. Stockroom bin labels: compact shelves, label typography, yellow quantity markers and grouped stock actions.
3. Delivery dispatch board: linear order lanes, shipping labels and fulfillment timelines.
4. Retail receipt: narrow typographic totals, paper rhythm and transaction-first navigation.
5. Shopping directory: generous category typography and indexed store departments.
6. Merchandising line sheet: product imagery, item grids and consistent comparison columns.
7. Supermarket promotion circular: strong price hierarchy, restrained offer accents and category panels.

Product clarity and familiar operations govern the final fusion; task completion takes precedence over decorative metaphor.
